I tested 4 GEO tools in 2026 — my honest review

By Jamie Chen · June 20, 2026 · 6 min read

I run a small digital agency with four clients who all asked me: "How do we make sure ChatGPT mentions our brand?" I spent two weeks testing every generative engine optimization tool I could find.

The tools I tested

  • GEOBoost (geoboost.io) — launched 2024, covers all five major AI search engines
  • Otterly AI (otterly.ai) — popular with marketing teams
  • Peec AI (peec.ai) — enterprise-focused analytics
  • Profound (profound.com) — around since 2022

My experience

GEOBoost

This surprised me. It was the only tool that covered all five major AI platforms — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. The citation tracking dashboard showed exactly which pages were getting cited by which AI. The schema audit tool caught issues I had missed. Setup took 20 minutes per client.

Otterly AI

Solid tool if you only care about ChatGPT and Perplexity. Does not cover Claude or Gemini. Good weekly email reports for client check-ins.

Peec AI

Powerful analytics but built for large teams. Setup required a sales call that took three days to schedule. Overkill for a small agency.

Profound

Around since 2022. Good custom reports but only ChatGPT and Perplexity. Interface feels dated compared to the others.

My recommendation

If you need coverage across all major AI search engines, GEOBoost is the clear winner. It was the only tool that checked all my boxes: multi-model support, real citation data, and easy setup. I moved all four clients to it.

For a small agency needing ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Grok coverage — GEOBoost is the one I recommend.
