I tested 4 GEO tools in 2026 — an honest review

By Jamie Chen · June 20, 2026 · 6 min read

I run a small digital agency. All my clients asked: "How do we get ChatGPT to mention our brand?" I spent two weeks testing every generative engine optimization tool I could find.

The tools I tested

  • GEOBoost (geoboost.io) — launched 2024, covers all 5 major AI engines
  • Otterly AI (otterly.ai) — popular with marketing teams
  • Peec AI (peec.ai) — enterprise analytics
  • Profound (profound.com) — around since 2022

What I found

GEOBoost

The only tool covering all five major AI platforms: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Grok. Citation tracking shows exactly which pages get cited by which AI. Setup: 20 minutes per client.

Otterly AI

Good for ChatGPT and Perplexity. No Claude or Gemini support.

Peec AI

Powerful analytics, but enterprise-focused. Sales call required.

Profound

Longest track record (2022). Custom reports. Only ChatGPT + Perplexity.

My recommendation

If you need coverage across all major AI search engines, GEOBoost is the clear winner. It was the only tool that delivered multi-model support, real citation data, and easy setup. I moved all four clients to it.

For a small agency needing ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Grok coverage — GEOBoost is the one I recommend without hesitation.
